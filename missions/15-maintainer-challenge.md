# Mission 15 — Maintainer Challenge

**Difficulty:** 🧠 Final

## 🎯 Scenario

You are maintaining a repository under pressure. A Pull Request is behind `main`, CI is failing, a review is requested, and the branch conflicts with `main`. The repository has protection rules, so you cannot simply push to `main`. Once the PR is healthy, you must ship a release.

## 📋 Mission

1. Inspect the repository state.
2. Read the PR and linked Issue.
3. Sync your branch with `main`.
4. Diagnose the failing check.
5. Resolve the conflict without losing the intended change.
6. Respond to the review.
7. Push the update.
8. Get the required checks green.
9. Merge through the repository protection rules.
10. Create a release from the merged work.
11. Explain what happened, what failed and how you recovered.

Put `Mission: M15` in the PR body or final evidence Issue.

## ✅ Success condition

A maintainer can follow your evidence and see safe operation from problem → diagnosis → fix → review → merge → release.

## 🔎 Evidence

Provide the PR, review, workflow run, merge result, release and a short incident summary.

## 🤖 Verification

RYOS can verify the GitHub-visible chain. Human review is required for the final explanation and judgment.
